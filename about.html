Introduction

The transportation and logistics industry is ever changing and is becoming even more sophisticated every year. Panther Empire Limited (PEL) is a third party logistics provider company that operates primarily within Kenya with services spanning across East Africa.

In order to best fulfill your needs, we offer services in contract logistics namely, Logistics, Transportation, Warehousing & Distribution and Product Supplies.

At PEL we are determined to continuously improve our service delivery methods and policies with the objective of exceeding client expectations and at the same time staying ahead of the competition.

About Us

  • Operations team with 25+ years combined industry experience
  • We offer free consulting to our clients regarding a variety of Logistics, Transport, Distribution processes and procedures
